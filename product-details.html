Experience sound exactly as it was intended. Beats Studio Buds are engineered to deliver powerful, balanced sound in a compact design. Through an entirely custom acoustic platform, the two-piece diaphragm driver resides within a dual-chamber housing, resulting in clear sound with outstanding stereo separation.

An advanced digital processor then optimizes audio performance for loudness and clarity, while simultaneously ensuring clean noise cancellation. Control the noise: Active Noise Cancelling (ANC) dynamically blocks unwanted external noise. Switch to Transparency mode when you need to hear the world around you. Microphones outside the earbuds mix the sounds of your surroundings back in with your music for a natural, ‘open’ listening experience.
