About Trands

Revolutionizing the way people interact with their IT, Tablet, and Mobile Phones.

Our Vision & Growth

Trands is one of the leading brands in IT, Tablet and Mobile accessory products in the world. Trands started with a vision to make accessories that revolutionize the way people interact with their IT, Tablet and Mobile Phones. Further, expanded the horizon by establishing itself in all GCC countries, beginning from United Arab Emirates.

Trands currently reached into a strong consumer base in other countries like North-Asia, China, India etc. Trands originated with 20 products and now it reached over 1000 products which is still increasing every month. Based on progressive features and higher innovations, and to fulfil our customer’s needs, our brand has diverse categories related to wired and wireless connectivity, adapters, chargers, power banks, gaming consoles, audio accessories etc.

Quality Commitment

Trands has always emphasized on quality and thus follow a strict policy in quality check. In addition, we guarantee our customers 1 year replacement warranty for all our products. We continue evolving and selling our phenomenal products to offer the opportunities for our customers to experience and enjoy the products we love.
